# Awesome MiniMax H3 Max

A curated collection of MiniMax H3 Max applications, tools, and resources, including free ways to try the model.

## What Is MiniMax H3 Max?

MiniMax H3 Max is an AI video generation model developed by fal Research based on MiniMax H3. It combines strong prompt adherence and high visual quality with ultra-fast inference, while supporting text-to-video and image-to-video generation with synchronized audio.

## MiniMax H3 Max Applications

Explore applications and projects powered by MiniMax H3 Max, from AI video creation tools to innovative creative workflows.

### Infinite Slop

🌐 https://infiniteslop.ai/

![infinite-slop](https://github.com/lmf12/ImageHost/blob/master/awesome-minimax-h3-max/infinite-slop.webp)

Infinite Slop is an endlessly evolving, AI-generated TV channel created by [@levelsio](https://x.com/levelsio) with fal.ai. Describe what you want to watch, and the service turns your idea into a continuously programmed video stream, letting viewers shape the next segment. It showcases MiniMax H3 Max’s rapid, prompt-responsive video generation for playful, unpredictable, community-driven entertainment experiences through a collaborative, always-on digital broadcast format.

### Fal Live

🌐 https://fal.live/

![fal-live](https://github.com/lmf12/ImageHost/blob/master/awesome-minimax-h3-max/fal-live.webp)

fal.live is a continuous AI-generated television experience powered by fal and MiniMax H3 Max. Explore live channels and direct what happens next, as audience prompts shape evolving stories, scenes, and interactive broadcasts in real time.

### Renoise Live

🌐 https://renoise.live/

![renoise-live](https://github.com/lmf12/ImageHost/blob/master/awesome-minimax-h3-max/renoise-live.webp)

Renoise Live is an AI-powered survival show that never stops filming. Follow four contestants stranded on a storm-hit island while the live chat decides what happens to them next, showcasing MiniMax-powered video generation in an interactive, continuously evolving broadcast.

### Interdimensional Game

🌐 https://github.com/blendi-remade/interdimensional-game

![interdimensional-game](https://github.com/lmf12/ImageHost/blob/master/awesome-minimax-h3-max/interdimensional-game.webp)

Interdimensional Game is an AI-powered interactive game project built around generative video experiences. It explores how MiniMax H3 Max can turn player prompts and choices into responsive, evolving visual worlds, demonstrating a playful use of fast, high-quality video generation for immersive, experimental gameplay.

### Live Classroom

🌐 https://github.com/internetphysics/live-classroom

![live-classroom](https://github.com/lmf12/ImageHost/blob/master/awesome-minimax-h3-max/live-classroom.webp)

Live Classroom is an AI-generated TV channel that teaches any topic you type. An LLM plans each one-minute lesson into twelve five-second beats, while MiniMax H3 Max Turbo renders 1970s-style educational-cartoon scenes just before they air on a CRT in a 3D classroom. Its program guide lets viewers queue the next lesson, showcasing just-in-time generative video for an interactive learning experience.
